# HD 82943

---

"This poster presents an updated dynamical analysis of HD 82943 - a system with two giant planets locked in a 2:1 orbital resonance, with periods of about 220 and 440 days. We combined nearly 15 years of HIRES and HARPS radial-velocity data with a self-consistent N-body model and a full Bayesian analysis, instead of a single best-fit point.

Our results strongly confirm the classical picture: a stable, coplanar, significantly inclined - about 17 degrees -resonant system, with true planet masses around three times their minimum values, roughly 5.4 and 5 Jupiter masses. Both resonant angles librate around zero degrees, which is direct evidence of a genuine, long-term stable resonance, confirmed by a 4000-year dynamical integration.

We also find a strong additional signal in the residuals, around 1100 days, which is consistent with a hypothesis raised back in 2014. We think a third planet is the most likely explanation, but we're not calling it confirmed yet, since we still need to rule out stellar activity."
